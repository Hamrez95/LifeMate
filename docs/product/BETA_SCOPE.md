# LifeMate invite-only beta scope

## Primary user
An employed adult aged 30–50 caring remotely for a parent with chronic medication needs.

## Core loop
1. Patient creates a treatment schedule.
2. WellMate reminds the patient.
3. Patient reports taken, skipped, or snoozed.
4. CareMate shows the reported status.
5. A missed-dose policy can notify the caregiver.
6. Patient can revoke caregiver access at any time.

## Included before Android beta
- Authentication and profile bootstrap
- Patient-caregiver invitation, consent, acceptance, listing, and revocation
- Medication and treatment schedule management
- Dose occurrences and adherence events
- WellMate and CareMate API integration
- Local reminders and controlled push-notification path
- Loading, error, offline, empty, and accessibility states
- Privacy, terms, consent, account deletion, audit, backup/export runbook
- Android release signing, versioning, APK/AAB generation, and internal testing

## Beta success evidence
- At least 10 families complete the core loop for four consecutive weeks.
- Activation and week-1/week-4 family retention are measured.
- Reminder-to-response latency and missed-dose follow-up are measured.
- Caregivers report reduced follow-up effort or anxiety.
- Patients do not experience unacceptable loss of autonomy or privacy.
- Willingness to pay is tested with a real offer, not only survey intent.
