# Coming soon policy

Out-of-scope capabilities remain in the LifeMate ecosystem without being presented as active functionality.

## Approved labels
- `به‌زودی`
- `در حال توسعه`
- `برای نسخه‌های آینده`

## Rules
- A coming-soon card must not look like a working form or destructive action.
- Tapping it may open a short explanation and optionally record anonymous interest.
- Do not promise a date unless the release is already scheduled and staffed.
- Do not collect health data for an unavailable capability.
- Do not claim clinical, diagnostic, prescription, emergency, or drug-interaction functionality.

## Initial coming-soon capabilities
- Coach and student
- Doctor and patient
- Professional nurse care
- Mother and child
- Pharmacy refill and purchase
- Drug-interaction intelligence
- AI assistant
- Lifetime unified health record
