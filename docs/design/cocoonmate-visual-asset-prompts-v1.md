# CocoonMate visual asset prompts v1

Status: implementation handoff. These assets are intentionally not generated in this coding pass. Each identifier is the stable Flutter replacement target.

## Shared art direction

Premium soft-3D editorial illustration for a Persian pregnancy and family-care app. Warm ivory background `#FFF8F2`, restrained coral `#FF6B5C`, soft sage, lilac and sky accents, clean rounded forms, gentle daylight, generous negative space, emotionally supportive, sophisticated rather than childish. No text, logo, UI, watermark, hospital scene, blood, glossy-plastic appearance or uncanny faces. Transparent PNG unless noted otherwise.

Purpose: primary pregnancy Home and Week Detail hero. Square 1:1; subject stays within the centre 82% with breathing room on the text-facing side.

Prompt: "Create a medically respectful soft editorial 3D illustration of fetal development at gestational week {WEEK}. A translucent abstract warm-coral amniotic halo may frame the fetus, but avoid anatomical cross-sections, organs, clinical-atlas rendering and graphic umbilical-cord detail. Preserve a calm side profile or curled posture with variation across weeks; never scale a copied silhouette. Use the shared CocoonMate art direction. Isolated transparent background, no text, labels or measurement marks."

Medical status: `medical review pending`. Do not present this art as clinical guidance without release-gate approval.
