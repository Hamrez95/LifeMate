# CocoonMate visual benchmark and fetal-reference synthesis

Audience: CocoonMate product, design, Flutter and clinical reviewers
Date: 2026-09-08
Scope: visual hierarchy and interaction strengths only; no competitor visual is
licensed for reuse and no competitor claim is accepted as clinical evidence.

## Executive answer

CocoonMate should lead with one calm pregnancy moment, a stage visual, week/day
and no more than three actions. Tracking tools belong after that emotional and
temporal orientation. Week Detail should read as an editorial story with
distinct mother, baby, routine-care and attention sections rather than repeated
cards. Partner access must remain consent-first and revocable. Generated fetal
art cannot ship before stage-specific evidence, proportion checks and a named
clinical review pass.

## Product evidence translated into decisions

- Flo combines week-by-week development, body changes, symptom tracking,
  checklist and partner sharing. CocoonMate adopts the concise orientation and
  bounded checklist, while separating clinical guidance from community or
  commercial content. Source: [Flo Pregnancy product tour](https://flo.health/product-tour/pregnancy-app),
  Flo Health, accessed 2026-09-08.
- Pregnancy+ makes its weekly 3D model the memorable interaction. CocoonMate
  should give the stage visual real visual weight but use short, optional motion
  and preserve care actions above the fold. Source: [Pregnancy+](https://philips-digital.com/pregnancy-new/),
  Philips Digital, accessed 2026-09-08.
- Ovia exposes appointments, notes and safety lookups alongside a stage-based
  experience. CocoonMate should keep an appointment preview compact and place
  lookup/search outside the emotional hero. Source: [Ovia Pregnancy](https://www.oviahealth.com/pregnancy-app/),
  Ovia Health, accessed 2026-09-08.
- BabyCenter and What to Expect demonstrate pregnancy-to-parenting continuity,
  rich weekly content and utilities. CocoonMate should keep articles and tools
  below the personal-care hierarchy and avoid turning Home into a feed. Sources:
  [BabyCenter app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/babycenter-track-pregnancy-app/id386022579)
  and [What to Expect app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pregnancy-baby-tracker-wte/id289560144),
  Everyday Health, accessed 2026-09-08.
- Clue's pregnancy mode gives body changes and fetal progress separate space.
  CocoonMate should do the same and keep language inclusive and clinically
  bounded. Source: [Clue Pregnancy](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/15007343040157-What-premium-features-are-part-of-Clue-Pregnancy),
  Clue, accessed 2026-09-08.
- Glow Nurture's useful pattern is track, understand, then optionally share.
  CocoonMate can provide clear post-entry feedback without treating a
  self-reported value as diagnosis. Source: [Glow Nurture](https://glowing.com/nurture-app),
  Glow, accessed 2026-09-08.
- amma frames the product around what matters now. CocoonMate should keep one
  “today focus” and put the tool collection in Quick Add. Source:
  [amma Pregnancy](https://amma.family/amma-benefits/pregnancy), amma, accessed
  2026-09-08.
- Peanut's relevant strength is stage-based connection and explicit community
  identity, not pregnancy tracking. If CocoonMate later adds community, expert
  content and peer advice must be visibly different. Source:
  [Peanut](https://www.peanut-app.io/), Peanut App Ltd, accessed 2026-09-08.
- Impo and Gahvareh show the value of Persian-first stage guidance, local tone,
  weekly care context and care-event reminders. CocoonMate should retain that
  cultural warmth with lower density and stronger clinical provenance. Sources:
  [Impo pregnancy](https://impo.app/landing/pregnancyLanding), ImPo, and
  [Gahvareh](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=pr.gahvare.gahvare),
  Gahvare Family, accessed 2026-09-08.
- HerLife demonstrates bite-sized daily stories and optional companion sharing.
  CocoonMate should keep daily insight short and sharing scoped. Source:
  [HerLife](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.herlife), Zarrino,
  accessed 2026-09-08.

## Medical visual evidence and release implication

- Carnegie staging is morphology-based and is not interchangeable with merely
  scaling one silhouette. The embryonic period through Carnegie stage 23 needs
  explicit head/body, limb, digit, posture and facial checks. Sources:
  [O'Rahilly and Müller](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20185898/), 2010, and
  the [NMHM Carnegie Collection](https://medicalmuseum.health.mil/index.cfm/collections/hdac/anatomy/index).
- The [3D Atlas of Human Embryology](https://www.3dembryoatlas.com/) provides
  high-confidence reference geometry for stages 7–23, but its CC BY-NC-ND
  terms mean it is a validation reference, not a source for commercial
  derivative artwork.
- General fetal milestones may be checked against
  [ACOG's fetal growth overview](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/how-your-fetus-grows-during-pregnancy).
  It is not sufficient alone for pose or exact proportion approval.
- Size and proportion statements should be treated as distributions, not exact
  personalized truth. Suitable validation sources are the
  [WHO fetal growth charts](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1002220)
  and [INTERGROWTH-21st standards](https://intergrowth21.tghn.org/standards-tools/).
- Dating and educational illustration are separate contracts. The
  [ISUOG fetal biometry guideline](https://www.isuog.org/static/d0d105b5-65b1-47f1-b4aa8b5e99afa1a6/ISUOG-Practice-Guidelines-ultrasound-fetal-biometry-growth.pdf)
  supports deriving gestational context from accepted dating data; a visual is
  never an ultrasound substitute.

## Limitations and stop condition

Official sources reliably expose feature sets and selected marketing screens,
but rarely document loading/offline states, accessibility, complete motion or
the current end-to-end flow. Those remain direct visual-QA tasks. Research
stopped when every requested product contributed at least one actionable,
non-copying pattern and the fetal release decision had multiple authoritative
references; further marketing-source collection was unlikely to change the
design direction.
